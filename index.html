Star Chef Linguini's Ratatouille

Chef Linguini's story to success is one struggle, he started off as a cleaning boy for Geausto's, a once famous restaurant now closed due to failing a health inspection, but after creating a delicious stew he was promptly promoted to a chef. During his career at Geasto's former Head Chef Skinner attempted to undermine Linguini's career, looking for any reason to fire him. The reason for this targeting was because Linguini is the son of Geausto, discovered in a letter written by Geausto himself, and was entitled to the restaurant. Despite Skinner's best attempts, nothing could squash Linguini's talent and he created the famous ratatouille that saved the restaurant. Unfortunately, Geausto's was promptly closed when a health inspector discovered a colony of rats on the premises. Despite the loss of Geasto's, Linguini has continued on opening his own restaurant, La Ratatouille, named after the famous dish that once won over the heart of infamous food critic Anton Ego. Linguini himself works as a waiter, possibly to let others find there time to shine. Still, his accomplishments are most impressive.

This recipe has been directly taken from Chef Linguini himself, who prior to this had remained silent on his recipes and how he created them. Linuini stated in the interview where he gave the recipe that "This is the true recipe of how to make my ratatouille, I can't hide it anymore, just read it and you'll see." It was such a heart felt statement and his belief that his knowledge and talent for cooking should not be witheld from the world has resonated with millions. As per the only wish of Chef Linguini, this recipe was directly written in this article by him, which he claims is to not to ruin the surprise for anyone. Once again a noble and kind thought that showcases his excellent moral virtues. Nothing more can be said of Chef Linguini, a man born with natural talent for cooking inherited from his father Chef Geausto, who also strived to overcome any obstacles in his way.
